Loopin is a guitar looper for your phone: record a base, play over it and keep adding tracks that always stay in time. Practise and play anywhere, with the phone's microphone or a USB audio interface.

ONE BUTTON, LIKE A PEDAL
• Tap: record, close the loop or overdub
• Hold: undo the last thing
• Double tap: stop and start
• Or use the volume buttons, the headset button or a Bluetooth pedal
• Stage mode: the whole screen is the button

ALWAYS IN TIME
• 4 tracks, each 1, 2 or 4 times the length of the first
• Metronome from 40 to 240 BPM with tap tempo and 10 time signatures (4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 7/8…)
• Synced, manual or free recording (the tempo comes from what you play)
• Start on play: recording begins with your first note
• Compensates your phone's delay: measured on its own in a few seconds

BUILT FOR PRACTICE
• Drums with 10 styles: rock, pop, blues, funk, reggae, waltz…
• Your own songs (WAV, MP3, FLAC) at any tempo, without changing the pitch
• Tempo trainer: raises the BPM step by step
• Chromatic tuner with reference notes to tune by ear

YOUR SESSIONS ARE SAFE
• Everything saves itself, like a notes app
• Export the mix or every track as WAV files for your DAW

SIMPLE OR ADVANCED
Simple mode keeps just the essentials (looper, metronome and tuner) with a bigger button. Advanced mode has everything.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese.

FREE AND PRIVATE
Free and open source software (GPLv3). No ads, no accounts and no internet: the app doesn't even have permission to use it. It only asks for the microphone, to record you.

Tip: through the phone speaker, the click and drums bleed into your recording. Wired headphones sound better; Bluetooth headphones have too much delay to monitor yourself live.
